## 3) Platform Adaptation Notes

### Amazon 版本
- Keep overlays minimal: max 3–6 words each.
- Prioritize: frame 1 (hero), 3 (dimensions), 4 (detail), 7 (in-box).
- Avoid dense collage; high legibility at mobile thumbnail size.

### Taobao/1688 版本
- Rich annotations acceptable: arrows, callouts, material labels.
- Add promotional rhythm: set value, display ideas, gifting angle.
- 1688/B2B 可追加 1 张批发信息图：装箱数、箱规、起订量（若有真实数据）。

---

## 4) Retouch Checklist
- Keep figurine contour and proportions faithful to source.
- Preserve color family and paint boundary style; avoid over-saturation.
- Remove dust/sensor noise only; do not alter sculpted facial geometry.
- Ensure dimension graphics are measured from actual sample.
- Packaging/content frame must match real shipped contents.
- Text claims must be factual and supportable.

---

## 5) A/B Variants for Main Conversion Hypothesis

### Hypothesis 1: “Single Hero clarity” vs “Set value perception”
- **A**: 单个主角摆件居中（更聚焦）
- **B**: 全套组合半圆排布（更强调价值感）
- KPI to observe: CTR, mobile zoom rate, add-to-cart rate.

### Hypothesis 2: “Neutral studio” vs “Warm home context” (detail frame)
- **A**: 全白棚拍风（理性）
- **B**: 轻家居场景（感性）
- KPI to observe: dwell time on image gallery, conversion lift by traffic source.
